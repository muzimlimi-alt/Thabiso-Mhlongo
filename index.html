    My Story

    From small-town beginnings to the big stage

    I’m Thabiso Victor Mhlongo, a Swati comedian, writer, actor, and TV presenter from Barberton, now hustling in Johannesburg. From small-town beginnings in August 2014 to shared stages with the GOATs!

    My humor thrives on sharp observations and stories everyone secretly nods at — school chaos, cultural quirks, everyday South African life. I flip between English and siSwati effortlessly, keeping my comedy conversational, witty, and unfiltered.

    Currently, I co-host People Need Comedy (PNC) on YouTube with the Podcast and Chill Network. I mix laughs with social commentary, giving people a reason to chuckle, reflect, and maybe even see themselves in the story.
