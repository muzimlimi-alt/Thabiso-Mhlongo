    My Story

    From small-town beginnings to the big stage

    Thabiso Victor Mhlongo here, a South African comedian, writer, actor, and TV presenter from Barberton, Mpumalanga, now hustling in Jozi (Johannesburg, Gauteng). From small-town beginnings in August 2014 to shared stages with the GOATs!

    My humour thrives on sharp observations and stories; everyone secretly nods at school chaos, cultural quirks, and everyday South African life. I flip between English and siSwati effortlessly, keeping my comedy conversational, witty, and unfiltered.

    Currently, I co-host People Need Comedy (PNC) on YouTube with the SABS Network. I mix laughs with social commentary, giving people a reason to chuckle, reflect, and maybe even see themselves in the story...

        POPIA Data Erasure

        Right to request anonymisation of your personal information
        What Is POPIA Data Erasure?

        Under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to request that we anonymise the personal information we hold about you. This form lets you submit that request — our team will review it and action it in line with our data retention obligations.

        What Gets Anonymised

        Once approved and processed, we replace your identifying details across our systems, including:

        • Your name, email address, phone number and company name on file.
        • Booking and enquiry records — personal details are replaced, while the record itself is kept for business continuity.
        • Newsletter subscription — removed entirely.
        • Contracts, quotes and internal notes — signatures and identifying content are redacted.
        • Communication and email logs referencing you.
        What We Must Retain

        South African tax and financial record-keeping law (SARS) requires certain records to be kept for a minimum of five years. Where this applies — invoices, transaction amounts, and financial ledger entries — we retain the record but strip out your personal identifiers wherever possible. Our internal audit log, which records administrative actions for compliance purposes, is also retained in full and is not itself subject to anonymisation.

        How This Affects You Going Forward
        • Future bookings: you'll need to submit your details again — we won't have a record to pre-fill or recognise you by.
        • Communication & support: our team won't be able to reference your booking history when assisting you.
        • Newsletter: you'll be unsubscribed and will need to opt in again if you'd like to hear from us.
        • Contracts & invoices: existing signed documents remain valid business records, but identifying content within them is redacted.

        This action cannot easily be reversed. Once your data has been anonymised, the original personal details cannot be recovered.

        Processing Time

        Requests are reviewed by our team and are typically processed within 5–10 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email with a reference number when you submit, and can quote this reference in any follow-up correspondence.

        Need Help?

        Questions about this process can be directed to bookings@thabisomhlongo.com. To read the legislation in full, see the Protection of Personal Information Act on gov.za.
